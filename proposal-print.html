1. Executive Summary

This proposal details the technical scope, phased development structure, professional fee, and third-party costs for a custom e-commerce web platform equipped with customer savings wallets and automated installment (credit) payment tracking, integrated with local payment processors (Paystack or Monnify).

Every monetary item in this document is assigned to exactly one of two owners so there is no ambiguity at any point:

1. The development fee — paid to the developer (Daniel Nicholas) for engineering the platform.
2. Third-party costs — paid directly by your organization to each provider (domain registrar, hosting, payment gateway, etc.), so your organization retains full ownership and administrative control of every digital asset.

4. Mandatory Prerequisites

  1. CAC business registration (required) — gate for live payment gateway verification.
  2. Business bank account (required) — settlement account in the registered entity's name.
  3. Verified merchant account (required) — live API keys for Paystack or Monnify.
  4. Written credit policy (required) — down-payment %, weekly vs monthly, grace period, penalties, interest.
  5. Product catalog data (required) — CSV/Excel with cash prices and installment breakdowns.
  6. KYC decision (required) — ID/BVN/guarantor rules before credit approval.
  7. Domain & hosting — purchased or owned by your organization (setup assistance available on your corporate email).
  8. Brand assets, website copy, terms of service and privacy policy documents.
  9. Official company email.

5. Timeline & Delivery

  • Milestone 1: 3–4 weeks from mobilization + prerequisites confirmation.
  • Milestone 2: 2–3 weeks from Milestone 1 acceptance.

The schedule pauses automatically if a prerequisite remains outstanding and resumes within 3 business days of it being provided. Where possible, delivery is made ahead of schedule.

6. Scope Management & Acceptance

  • Each milestone includes two rounds of client review; each review window is five business days. Silence after five business days counts as acceptance of that milestone.
  • Business-rule changes raised mid-build (repayment schedules, penalties, interest, product flows) are new scope, quoted separately before implementation.

7. Optional Post-Launch Maintenance & Support Retainer

₦35,000 / month (optional) — covers uptime monitoring, security patching, monthly transaction-ledger health checks, and routine support with a one-business-day response commitment. Explicitly excludes new feature development (quoted separately as needed).
