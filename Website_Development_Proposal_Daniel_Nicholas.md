## 1. Executive Summary

This proposal details the technical scope, phased development structure, professional fee, and third-party costs for a custom e-commerce web platform equipped with **customer savings wallets** and **automated installment (credit) payment tracking**, integrated with local payment processors (Paystack or Monnify).

Every monetary item in this document is assigned to exactly one of two owners so there is no ambiguity at any point:

- **The development fee** — paid to me, Daniel Nicholas, for engineering the platform.
- **Third-party costs** — paid directly by your organization to each provider (domain registrar, hosting, payment gateway, etc.), so your organization retains full ownership and admin control of every digital asset.

## 2. Scope of Work and Phased Development Structure

Development is structured into **two milestones**, each independently valuable, testable, and separately paid so that progress is always visible and risk is always shared.

### Milestone 1 — E-Commerce Core & Storefront — ₦350,000

| What your customers get | What your staff get |
|---|---|
| Professional storefront and product catalog | Core admin dashboard: products, orders, users |
| Secure customer registration and login | One bulk product import from your catalog file |
| Direct checkout via Paystack or Monnify | Order and payment status tracking |

**Delivery:** 3–4 weeks from mobilization fee + confirmed prerequisites.

### Milestone 2 — Financial Engine: Savings Wallet & Installment Tracking — ₦350,000

| What your customers get | What your staff get |
|---|---|
| Savings wallet: save toward any purchase, watch progress | Wallet & ledger admin screens with full transaction history |
| Pay-by-installment purchases (weekly or monthly schedules) | Automated repayment tracking, status and reminders |
| Automatic payment reminders | Credit repayment monitoring and reporting |

> **Architecture note.** Customer funds flow directly through the payment gateway to your organization's own settlement account. The system records and reconciles customer claims without holding pooled funds — intentionally keeping your operation outside the scope of CBN payment-servicing licensing for this use case.

**Delivery:** 2–3 weeks from acceptance of Milestone 1.

## 4. Mandatory Prerequisites (before development begins)

1. **CAC business registration** (REQUIRED) — gate for live payment gateway verification.
2. **Business bank account** (REQUIRED) — settlement account in the registered entity's name.
3. **Verified merchant account** (REQUIRED) — live API keys for Paystack or Monnify.
4. **Written credit policy** (REQUIRED) — down-payment %, weekly vs monthly, grace period, penalties, interest.
5. **Product catalog data** (REQUIRED) — CSV/Excel with cash prices and installment breakdowns.
6. **KYC decision** (REQUIRED) — ID/BVN/guarantor rules before credit approval.
7. Domain & hosting — purchased or owned by your organization (assistance available on your corporate email).
8. Brand assets, website copy, terms of service and privacy policy documents.
9. Official company email.
